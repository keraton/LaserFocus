# Welcome to LaserFocus

LaserFocus keeps your attention on **t thing at a time**. The text you are writing stays bright while everything else fades into the background.

## Focus modes

Use the *View* menu to choose how much stays in focus: a single sentence, one paragraph, two paragraphs or three.

> Good writing is rewriting.

### Shortcuts

- Press `⌘1` to focus on a sentence.
- Press `⌘2` to focus on a paragraph.
- Turn on grammar colors with ⌥⌘G.
- Show this outline with ⌃⌘S.
